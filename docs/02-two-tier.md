# 02 — Two-Tier Campus Topology

## Objective

Expand the initial campus into a two-tier switching design and introduce the redundant uplink conditions required for later STP and EtherChannel work.

## Focus

- Access/distribution structure
- Redundant uplinks
- Layer-2 forwarding and verification

## Progression

This stage provides the physical foundation for VLAN segmentation and STP behavior explored in the next labs.
